About SceneFuse

SceneFuse helps you combine selected photos into finished image composites, crop results, and save finished images to your photo library.

Purchases and Subscriptions

SceneFuse offers optional subscriptions for unlimited full-resolution saves and an optional one-time save in-app purchase. Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store payment system.

If a purchase does not appear immediately, open SceneFuse and use Restore Purchases. You can also manage subscriptions from your Apple ID subscription settings.

Photo Library Access

SceneFuse needs photo library access so you can choose images and save finished results. You can manage photo access in the iOS Settings app.

Privacy

SceneFuse does not collect personal information or upload your photos to our servers. For more details, please see the SceneFuse Privacy Policy.
